LV Power Factor Correction

LV Detuned Reactors for Power Factor Correction Systems

Detuned reactors are used in power factor correction systems to prevent resonance and protect capacitors in networks affected by harmonic distortion. Available in different tuning factors and design levels, they ensure reliable operation in industrial environments.

QUICK SELECTION

Select the Right Tuning Factor

Start here. Match your network condition and harmonic environment to the recommended tuning factor and design level, then review the product configurations below.

Network Condition Typical Scenario Recommended Tuning
Clean network, no significant harmonic sources Standard industrial or commercial installation 5.67%
Industrial network with VFDs or variable speed drives Manufacturing, pumping, HVAC with inverters 7%
Mixed harmonic sources, moderate distortion — including 3rd harmonic presence Mixed loads, multiple drive types, single-phase loads, welding 14%
Highly stressed network, severe harmonic environment Based on harmonic analysis On request

Detuning factor defines the resonance frequency of the LC circuit, not the harmonic level in the network. Reactor design level determines its ability to withstand harmonic stress, overloads and thermal demands.

Final selection should be based on a harmonic measurement or analysis of the installation. Contact our technical team if measurement data is available.

INSTALLATION RISK

What Happens If the Wrong Reactor Is Selected

Using a reactor with incorrect tuning factor or insufficient design level can compromise the protection of the capacitor bank and the stability of the entire power factor correction system.

Resonance amplification causing voltage and current distortion to increase rather than decrease

Reactor overheating due to insufficient thermal design for the actual harmonic load

Premature failure of the capacitor bank caused by resonance or overcurrent

Acoustic noise and vibration from reactor saturation in harmonic-rich environments

Unstable operation of the PFC system and loss of reactive power compensation

APPLICATIONS

Typical Applications

LV detuned reactors are used in power factor correction systems wherever harmonic distortion is present or expected in the low-voltage network.

Industrial plants and manufacturing facilities with variable speed drives

Automatic and fixed PFC switchboards in harmonic environments

Installations with VFDs, UPS systems and non-linear loads

HVAC systems with inverter-driven compressors and fans

Data centres and commercial buildings with mixed electrical loads

Heavy industrial environments with high harmonic stress and demanding duty
