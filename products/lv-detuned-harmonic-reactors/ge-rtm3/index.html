When to Use

When to Use GE-RTM3 Standard

GE-RTM3 Standard is the correct choice for new PFC systems in clean or moderately distorted networks where ambient temperature does not exceed 40 °C and harmonic stress is within standard operating limits. Reactor and capacitor are engineered together as a matched LC step, eliminating mismatch risk and ensuring accurate kVAr output.

  • New automatic or fixed power factor correction systems designed from scratch
  • Networks with limited harmonic distortion and stable operating conditions
  • Applications requiring accurate kVAr output per compensation step
  • Installations with 5th or 7th harmonic components within standard limits
  • Systems where reactor and capacitor tolerances must be controlled together

GE-RTM3 units are supplied as matched reactor-capacitor combinations. The following table allows preliminary selection of LC steps based on system voltage, frequency and tuning factor. Capacitor voltage is automatically increased due to detuned reactor operation, the minimum required capacitor voltage is shown for each configuration.

Important: Capacitor voltage is higher than nominal system voltage due to detuned reactor operation. The value shown represents the minimum required capacitor voltage rating, always select the nearest higher available standard capacitor voltage. Inductance values are calculated for the matched LC step at the selected conditions.

All values calculated for three-phase operation at selected voltage and frequency. Inductance and capacitance are interdependent values are valid only for the GE-RTM3 matched LC step. Do not use inductance values to retrofit existing capacitor banks, use GE-RT3 for retrofit applications.

Part Number

Part Number Structure

The GE-RTM3 part number identifies the product family, power rating, system voltage and resonance frequency.

GE3RTM[kVAr].[Voltage]R[Resonance Hz]

Example: GE3RTM25.400R189 — 25 kVAr LC step, 400 V, resonance 189 Hz (7% at 50 Hz).
Example: GE3RTM10.400R210 — 10 kVAr LC step, 400 V, resonance 210 Hz (5.67% at 50 Hz).
Example: GE3RTM50.690R134 — 50 kVAr LC step, 690 V, resonance 134 Hz (14% at 50 Hz).
