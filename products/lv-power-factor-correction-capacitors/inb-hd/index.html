INB HD Power Factor Correction Capacitors

Premium heavy-duty low-voltage capacitors for demanding industrial power factor correction systems with increased harmonic current stress and switching duty.

NLL < 20% Typical THDi ≤ 15% Typical THDu ≤ 6% Service life 165,000 h
WHEN TO USE

When to Use INB HD Capacitors

INB HD capacitors are designed for industrial power factor correction systems with higher harmonic current stress and more demanding operating conditions compared to standard industrial applications.

  • Industrial installations with increased harmonic distortion
  • Automatic capacitor banks with frequent switching operations
  • Networks with VFDs, UPS systems and non-linear loads
  • Applications requiring higher thermal and electrical robustness
  • Industrial environments with demanding operating conditions

PART NUMBER

Part Number Structure

The INB HD part number identifies configuration, power, frequency and voltage. The final suffix *** is completed according to capacitor dimensions and terminal configuration. For single-phase execution, the prefix becomes 1PF.

3PF[kVar][Freq][Voltage]BHD[***]
1PF[kVar][Freq][Voltage]BHD[***]

Example: 3PF10A400BHD*** — 10 kVar, 50 Hz (A), 400 V, INB HD series, three-phase.
Example: 1PF10A400BHD*** — same rating, single-phase execution.
Suffix *** defines dimensions and terminals.

SELECTION LIMITS

When a More Robust Series May Be Required

If any of the following conditions apply to your installation, a higher-performance series such as INP HD or INA HD should be considered.

  • Measured THDi above 15% or THDu above 6%
  • NLL greater than 20%
  • High presence of VFDs, UPS or rectifier loads
  • Heavy switching duty in automatic capacitor banks
  • Strong harmonic current stress
  • Demanding industrial environments beyond INB HD operating range
