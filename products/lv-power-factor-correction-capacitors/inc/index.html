INC Power Factor Correction Capacitors

Industrial low-voltage capacitors for automatic power factor correction systems, designed for general industrial networks with moderate harmonic stress.

NLL < 17% Typical THDi ≤ 12% Typical THDu ≤ 5% Service life 140,000 h
WHEN TO USE

When to Use INC Capacitors

INC capacitors are designed for industrial low-voltage power factor correction systems where higher operating robustness, wider voltage range and incorporated fuse protection are required.

  • General industrial power factor correction systems
  • Automatic capacitor banks in industrial networks
  • Installations with moderate harmonic distortion
  • Networks with variable loads and non-linear load presence
  • Applications requiring incorporated fuse protection

PART NUMBER

Part Number Structure

The INC part number identifies configuration, power, frequency and voltage. The final suffix *** is completed according to capacitor dimensions and terminal configuration. For single-phase execution, the prefix becomes 1PF.

3PF[kVar][Freq][Voltage]INC[***]

Example: 3PF10A400INC*** — 10 kVar, 50 Hz (A), 400 V, INC series, three-phase.
Example: 1PF10A400INC*** — same rating, single-phase execution.
Suffix *** defines dimensions and terminals.

SELECTION LIMITS

When a More Robust Series May Be Required

If any of the following conditions apply to your installation, a more robust series such as INB HD, INP HD or INA HD should be considered.

  • Measured THDi above 12% or THDu above 5%
  • NLL greater than 17%
  • Heavy VFD, UPS or rectifier presence with significant harmonic injection
  • Frequent switching duty in demanding automatic capacitor banks
  • High harmonic current stress in industrial networks
  • Critical industrial networks with elevated electrical or thermal stress
